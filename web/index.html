SENSITIVITY

Recalibrate with the room empty and still (10 s). Sensitivity is saved on the board.

How it works: the board measures 51 WiFi subcarriers of every frame your router sends it (~50+ per second) and compares how much they fluctuate against the quiet-room baseline. "VS BASELINE" 1.0× = like the empty room. Moving people, pets or fans raise it. It cannot see, count, or locate anyone.

HOW TO SURVEY

1. Draw your rooms (▭ ROOMS: drag a rectangle, then name it) or load a photo of a floor plan (🖼).
2. Power the board from a USB power bank and carry it with your phone (phone on the same home WiFi).
3. In 📍 SURVEY, stand still, tap your spot on the plan: the board averages 10 RSSI readings (~1.5 s).
4. Cover each room; the map interpolates between points (IDW) and fades out where there is no data.
Plan + points are saved in this browser only (not on the board). The ESP32-S3 is 2.4 GHz only — this maps 2.4 GHz coverage, not 5 GHz/6 GHz.

DEVICES ON YOUR LAN

FOUND

Tap SCAN NETWORK (takes ~15 s).

ARP sweep of your home /24 from the board. Vendor comes from the MAC prefix (a built-in table of common brands); phones usually use a private (randomized) MAC per network, so they show no vendor. Names come from NetBIOS when a device answers. Devices that sleep or ignore ARP may be missed. Only scan networks you own or are allowed to scan.
