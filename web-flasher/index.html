ESP RADAR · WEB FLASHER

Flash the Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4 straight from Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial). Nothing to install.

  1. Plug the board in with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click CONNECT, pick the board's port, then Install.
  3. Updating from v1? Leave Erase device unticked to keep your saved WiFi.
  4. If no port appears / it won't connect: hold PRG, tap RST, release PRG, try again.

Your browser has no Web Serial. Use desktop Chrome or Edge. This page must be opened over https:// or http://localhost.

Afterwards: join WiFi ESP-Radar-XXXX (password espradar) and open http://192.168.71.1.
