BETA · SMART LIGHTSoff by default

Discovery sends a unicast probe to each host already on the Devices list (Govee UDP 4001, WiZ 38899, LIFX 56700), and still sends multicast and broadcast as well. eero often drops broadcast between mesh nodes; unicast is the path that still works. Govee LAN Control is per device in the Govee Home app, and only some Govee models support it — zero Govee replies after a unicast sweep means those bulbs are not in LAN mode, not that the packet was lost. Tuya and CloudyBay show up as “found, needs local key”; the key stays on the board and is never shown again. Untested on hardware.

H6008 (and other Govee bulbs with no LAN Control switch) can be driven over Bluetooth instead. The board uses the ESP32-S3 NimBLE stack, scans only when you ask (about 4 seconds, and the WiFi motion rate will dip for that long), then connects just long enough to write one on/off or brightness command. No keep-alive timer, and no Govee cloud. Practical range is about one or two rooms. Untested on hardware.

HOW TO SURVEY

1. Draw your rooms (▭ ROOMS: drag a rectangle, then name it) or load a photo of a floor plan (🖼).
2. Power the board from a USB power bank and carry it with your phone (phone on the same home WiFi).
3. In 📍 SURVEY, stand still, tap your spot on the plan: the board averages 10 RSSI readings (~1.5 s).
4. Cover each room; the map interpolates between points (IDW) and fades out where there is no data.
Plan + points are saved in this browser only (not on the board). The ESP32-S3 is 2.4 GHz only — this maps 2.4 GHz coverage, not 5 GHz/6 GHz.

SECURITY AUDITpen-test-lite

Light check of devices on your own /24: common ports, banners, a few risk flags, and an optional capped default-login check. Not nmap or Metasploit — it can miss things and occasionally mis-flag. No exploits, no password cracking beyond the small default list.

FOUND

Tap SCAN NETWORK (takes ~15 s).

ARP sweep of your home /24 from the board, plus a slow background pass for online/offline. Uptime, last dropout, flapping (lots of up/down changes) and overnight activity stay in a small log on the board. Vendor comes from the MAC prefix; phones usually use a private (randomized) MAC, so they show no vendor. A device with no vendor that just joined is marked unknown. No port scan was added for this. Only scan networks you own.
