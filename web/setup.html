ESP RADAR · WIFI

Connect the radar to your home WiFi (2.4 GHz). It needs a link to your router: motion is sensed from the router's signal (WiFi CSI). After saving, the board restarts. You can still reach it at 192.168.4.1 on this setup network, or at http://esp-radar.local / its IP (shown on the OLED) from your home WiFi.
